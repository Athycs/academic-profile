Overview

I am a researcher with academic training in public health, medical big data, epidemiology and human nutrition. My research examines how lifestyle behaviours, existing health conditions and socioeconomic circumstances relate to mortality and healthy ageing.

I have worked with the Korean Genome and Epidemiology Study, using longitudinal cohort data to study combined lifestyle behaviours, morbidity and years of life lost. My broader interests include health inequalities, disease prevention and the evaluation of artificial intelligence in primary care.

Before my postgraduate training, I worked in community nutrition and public health programming in Uganda, including contributions to district nutrition action planning.
